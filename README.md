# HR Connect AI

### Intelligent Employee Onboarding & HR Operations Support System

**Simplifying Onboarding. Empowering People.**

HR Connect AI is an AI-enabled HR technology project designed to bring greater structure, accessibility and efficiency to employee onboarding and routine HR support.

### Project Highlights
- **AI-Driven Research:** NotebookLM-supported business research and insights.
- **Intelligent HR Assistance:** Customised Gemini Gem for routine HR guidance.
- **Mobile Application:** Android-oriented onboarding and HR support prototype.
- **Guided Onboarding:** Structured task tracking and documentation support.
- **Progress Visibility:** Organised onboarding updates and reporting.

### Development Approach
NotebookLM • Google Gemini • Google AI Studio • Android Application Development

### Vision
To make HR processes more connected, accessible and employee-centric through purposeful technology and responsible AI.
